# 02 — VISUAL & EXPERIENCE VISION

# Concept
## **Những điều rất nhỏ có thể mang một ý nghĩa rất lớn.**

The cover already contains the visual metaphor:
- a grandmother beside a child at a piano;
- a piano opening into an abundant vertical garden;
- floating sheets/pages;
- quiet ivory negative space;
- growth emerging from persistence and accompaniment.

The website should extend that visual metaphor without copying the cover mechanically.

---

# Visual DNA

## Background
Warm ivory / paper:
- approximately the emotional register of the cover, not pure white;
- subtle texture only if almost imperceptible.

## Primary ink
Deep blue/navy taken from the title and cover typography.

## Botanical palette
Derived from the actual cover:
- moss/forest green;
- soft leaf green;
- sky/flower blue;
- lily orange;
- coral/poppy pink;
- muted olive;
- charcoal/black for photography/text.

Extract exact tokens from the actual cover during implementation.

## Visual contrast system

Two visual languages should coexist:

### COVER WORLD
- color;
- watercolor;
- botanical abundance;
- piano;
- hope and growth.

### MEMORY / DOCUMENTARY WORLD
- black-and-white family photographs;
- generous white space;
- book-page typography;
- real moments.

This contrast is more persuasive than adding decorative marketing illustrations.

---

# Typography

Use no more than two families:
- literary serif with strong Vietnamese for headings/body where appropriate;
- restrained sans or serif companion for navigation/meta/controls.

Do not attempt to imitate the cover's stylized lettering with a cheap decorative font.

Typography should feel:
- literary;
- intimate;
- mature;
- humane.

Not:
- wellness startup;
- hospital website;
- charity campaign;
- parenting blog template.

---

# Composition

Editorial and asymmetrical.

Allow:
- large cover;
- real photos;
- botanical crops from the cover;
- narrow text measures;
- pull quotes;
- subtle rules.

Avoid:
- card soup;
- many rounded boxes;
- gradients;
- glassmorphism;
- stock photos;
- icon-heavy feature grids;
- medical blue UI;
- before/after comparison layouts.

---

# Motion

Very restrained:
- subtle image reveal;
- 4–8px cover lift;
- gentle botanical parallax only if it survives performance and reduced-motion QA.

No:
- floating flowers continuously;
- confetti;
- autoplay carousel;
- emotional “heartbeat” effects.

Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
