# DESIGN

Use cover-derived:
- ivory;
- deep blue;
- botanical watercolor colors;
- editorial whitespace.

Use real:
- cover;
- author portrait;
- documentary photos.

Avoid:
- stock imagery;
- AI imagery;
- autism puzzle-piece cliché;
- medical template aesthetics;
- glass;
- gradients;
- card soup;
- excessive rounded UI.

Preserve image ratios.
Do not distort or exploit documentary photographs.
