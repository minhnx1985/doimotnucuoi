# 06 — MARKETING, PSYCHOLOGY & SAFETY RULES

## Conversion objective

Optimize for:
**qualified purchase clicks**

Not merely:
- scroll depth;
- emotional intensity;
- CTA count.

## Ethical persuasion framework

### Recognition
Start with a real, relatable observation.

### Narrative
Use the real grandmother–grandchild story.

### Specificity
Use:
- more than ten years;
- chapter structure;
- photographs;
- author identity.

### Credibility
Separate:
- legal/professional credentials;
- lived family experience;
- medical/scientific expertise.

Never merge these categories.

### Demonstration
The sample PDF reduces uncertainty better than more adjectives.

### Friction reduction
Keep the purchase CTA obvious and repeated at high-intent points.

### Consistency
Same CTA destination and label site-wide.

### Cognitive fluency
Simple navigation.
Short section purpose.
No clutter.

# Sensitive-health content guardrails

Autism is health/disability-related sensitive subject matter.

Do NOT market with:
- fear of a child's future;
- parental guilt;
- shame;
- “normal vs abnormal” framing as the site's voice;
- cure claims;
- recovery guarantees;
- causal claims;
- treatment recommendations;
- medical advice.

Do NOT use:
- “chữa khỏi”;
- “thoát tự kỷ”;
- “đánh bại tự kỷ”;
- “biến con thành bình thường”.

If source text contains older or contested language:
do not amplify it in marketing copy.

Prefer:
- “trẻ trong phổ tự kỷ” where natural;
- “trẻ tự kỷ” when referring faithfully to book section titles/source wording;
- “đồng hành”;
- “hòa nhập”;
- “môi trường phù hợp”;
- “mỗi trẻ em và mỗi gia đình khác nhau”.

Do not imply the book can replace:
- doctors;
- psychologists;
- therapists;
- special-education professionals.

# UX persuasion rules

Allowed:
- sticky purchase CTA;
- repeated purchase CTA;
- real social/context proof;
- author authority;
- preview;
- short pull quotes.

Forbidden:
- fake countdown;
- fake scarcity;
- exit-intent modal;
- forced email capture;
- dark patterns;
- pre-checked consent;
- autoplay emotional video/audio;
- fabricated star ratings;
- fabricated reader testimonials.
