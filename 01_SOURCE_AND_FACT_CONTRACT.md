# 01 — SOURCE & FACT CONTRACT

## A. Book identity — LOCKED

Title:
**Đợi một nụ cười**

Subtitle:
**Hành trình của một người bà đồng hành cùng cháu tự kỷ**

Author:
**Nguyễn Thị Quỳnh Anh**

The source material presents the book as a true account of more than ten years in which a grandmother accompanies her grandson Vũ Nhật Minh after autism-spectrum-related difficulties emerge in early childhood.

Do not convert this into a medical success story.

## B. Narrative ground truth from source material

The back-cover/source copy establishes:
- for many families, a smile or very small change may appear ordinary;
- in this family's experience, such moments may represent long periods of waiting, experimentation and persistence;
- the journey begins with difficult questions about autism, changes in the child's behavior and the future;
- the book follows more than ten years of the grandmother accompanying Nhật Minh;
- the journey includes hospitals, schools, early difficulties, gradual progress, inclusion and people who supported the family;
- the broader social concern includes understanding, compassion and inclusive education.

The sample's table of contents establishes five large arcs:

1. **Khi thế giới bỗng trở nên im lặng**
2. **Khi cô giáo không phải mẹ hiền**
3. **Những người mở lại cánh cửa**
4. **Những vòng tay rộng mở**
5. **Không để ai bị bỏ lại phía sau**

The book closes with:
- **Tâm sự của bà**
- a postscript on accompanying autistic children from early intervention to an inclusive educational environment.

## C. Important nuance

The source itself explicitly recognizes:
- every child is different;
- no one can give certain answers to every family's questions;
- the environment, family, teachers and supporting people matter;
- the story is one family's lived experience.

This nuance must survive marketing compression.

Nguyễn Thị Quỳnh Anh:
- lawyer;
- more than 30 years of legal-consulting experience according to supplied jacket copy;
- Vice President of the Vietnam Bar Federation;
- Director of InvestPro Law Firm according to supplied jacket copy;
- involved in cultural/friendship activities between Vietnam and other countries;

Current public-source verification also supports that she remains Vice President of the Vietnam Bar Federation in 2026.

## E. Author-authority rule

IMPORTANT:

Her professional/legal credentials establish public credibility and identity.

They do **NOT** make her:
- a doctor;
- a psychologist;
- an autism clinician;
- a therapist;
- a scientific authority on autism.

The landing page must present her authority here primarily as:
> **the grandmother who lived this experience and chose to document it**, alongside her established professional/public profile.

Never imply clinical expertise she does not claim.

## F. Photographic evidence

The sample contains real family photographs.

Notable included visual evidence:
- a black-and-white photograph of a smiling child playing in water;
- a black-and-white photograph of Nguyễn Thị Quỳnh Anh holding Minh at age one.

These real images are high-value trust assets.

Use with dignity.
Do not over-dramatize, crop exploitatively or turn the child into a “before/after” advertisement.

## G. Source priority

For public book copy:
1. supplied DOCX;
2. supplied sample PDF;
3. supplied cover;
4. authoritative current public sources for author role verification;
5. no model-memory fact filling.

If unsupported:
omit it.
